## Description

Markup is a privacy-first markdown editor built for developers, writers, and anyone who wants their words to stay their own.

**What you get:**
- 🎨 **7 themes** — From clean light to deep dark. Pick your vibe.
- ☁️ **Cloud sync** — Your notes follow you across devices.
- 💳 **Stripe billing** — Upgrade seamlessly when you're ready.
- 🔌 **Chrome extension** — Capture ideas without leaving your tab.
- 📝 **Pure markdown** — No lock-in. Just `.md` files you own.

No tracking. No analytics creep. Just a fast, beautiful editor that respects your privacy.

Markup is part of the **Ninja Money Machine** suite — 4 products built by orchestrating AI agent swarms in a single sprint. Check out the full portfolio at https://alifromtheends.github.io/ninja-money-machine/

Markup was the anchor product. I needed a tool I actually wanted to use: something fast, private, and themeable. The AI agents suggested the Chrome extension. They proposed the Stripe integration. They even wrote the first draft of this very post.
