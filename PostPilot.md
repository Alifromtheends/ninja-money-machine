> AI-powered social media content scheduler. Plans your calendar, writes posts, auto-generates hashtags, and publishes them.

## What It Does
- Multi-platform composer (Twitter/X, LinkedIn, Instagram, Facebook, TikTok)
- AI auto-generates hashtags based on content
- Smart schedule picker with optimal time recommendations
- Content calendar view
- Analytics dashboard with engagement charts
- Auto-saves to localStorage
